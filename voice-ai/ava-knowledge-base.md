## How leasing works (plain English)
Leasing = paying for the use of a vehicle over a set term (commonly 24–39 months) with an
annual mileage allowance, instead of buying it. Monthly cost depends on the vehicle, term,
mileage, and current manufacturer programs — which change monthly, so we always confirm the
latest before sharing options.

## Auto broker vs. dealership
A dealership represents its own inventory and goals. A broker works for the customer —
organizing the search and coordinating with dealer partners so it's built around them. Best
for people who value their time and dislike the showroom dance.

## Credit (honest framing)
- We help across credit situations, but we never promise approval — approval comes from a
  lender/dealer.
- For lower credit, leasing can be limited; we'll review the person's real options honestly
  and point them to the right path (which may be finance instead of lease). We don't over-
  promise.
